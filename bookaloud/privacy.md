# BookAloud privacy policy

Effective 11 November 2026.

BookAloud reads printed books aloud: you photograph the pages, and the app recognizes the text
and reads it with an Apple voice. It is made by one person, has no servers, does not connect to
the internet, and does not collect any information about you.

## What stays on your device

When you add a book, BookAloud stores these on your iPhone or iPad only:

- the photos you take or choose of the book's pages and cover,
- the text recognized from those photos, including any corrections you make,
- the book's title and language, and the voice you picked for it,
- your reading settings, such as speed and text size.

None of it is sent anywhere. Like other app data, it may be included in your device's iCloud or
computer backups, depending on your device settings; those backups are managed by Apple and by
you.

## Camera and photos

BookAloud asks for the camera only so you can photograph book pages and covers. When you import
from your photo library, the app sees only the photos you pick.

## Text recognition and speech

Recognizing the text in your photos and reading it aloud both happen on your device, using
Apple's built-in Vision framework and system voices. Nothing is uploaded for processing.

## Sharing a book

If you choose Share Book, BookAloud puts that book's photos and text into one file and opens
Apple's share sheet. The file goes only where you send it, for example by AirDrop to another
device. Once sent, it is handled by the method you chose (AirDrop, Messages, Mail, Files and so
on). BookAloud never shares anything on its own.

## Deleting your data

Long-press a book in your library and choose Delete Book to remove it and its photos. Deleting
the app deletes everything it stored.

## What BookAloud does not do

No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party code, no data sold or
shared.

## Children

BookAloud is meant for parents and other adults who read to children. It collects no personal
information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
