# BookAloud support

Questions, bugs or ideas: email [llrr.support@gmail.com](mailto:llrr.support@gmail.com). Please include your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.

**I can't choose my book's language, or the app says no Premium voice is installed.**
BookAloud only uses Apple's Premium voices, because they sound the most natural. They are free
but have to be downloaded once: open Settings > Accessibility > Read & Speak > Voices (on iOS 17
and 18: Spoken Content > Voices), choose the language, then a voice marked Premium (for French,
Audrey is a good choice), and tap the download icon. Voices are a few hundred MB, so use Wi-Fi.
The Voices button in BookAloud's library shows these steps and lists the voices you have.

**A word is read wrong.** Text recognition sometimes misreads a word. Tap the pencil icon while
reading to correct the text; the page photo stays visible while you type.

**The scanner stopped after 24 pages.** Apple's scanner takes up to 24 pages at a time. Tap Scan
more pages to continue, then Save. You can also add pages later: long-press the book in your
library and choose Add Pages.

**Opening a book someone shared with me.** A book sent by AirDrop lands in the Files app
(Downloads). Tap it there to open it in BookAloud, or use the Import button in BookAloud's library.

**Unlimited Books and restoring a purchase.** Your first 3 books are free; after that,
Unlimited Books is a one-time purchase (no subscription). With Family Sharing turned on, everyone
in your family gets it. On a new device, or if the app ever asks you to buy again, open Settings
in BookAloud (gear icon) and tap Restore Purchase, signed in with the Apple Account that bought it.
For refunds, use Apple's [Report a Problem](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) page.
