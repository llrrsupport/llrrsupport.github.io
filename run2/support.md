# Run2 support

Questions, bugs or ideas: email [llrr.support@gmail.com](mailto:llrr.support@gmail.com). Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

**My runs are not counted.** Open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps > Run2,
and turn everything on. Run2 counts only Running workouts for run journeys and Cycling workouts
for bike journeys, recorded on or after the journey's start date. Walks do not count.
