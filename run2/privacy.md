# Run2 privacy policy

Effective 31 October 2026.

Run2 turns the runs and rides you already record into a journey across a map. It is made by
one person, has no servers, and does not collect any information about you.

## Apple Health

- Run2 reads running and cycling workouts and their distances from Apple Health, only to work
  out how far along your journeys you are.
- When you add a run or ride by hand, Run2 saves it to Apple Health as a workout, so your other
  apps see it too. Run2 can delete only workouts it saved itself.
- Your health data stays on your phone. Run2 never sends it anywhere, never uses it for
  advertising, and never shares it with anyone.
- You can change or withdraw Run2's access at any time in the Health app: tap your profile
  picture, then Apps > Run2.

## Journeys

Your journeys, their routes and your progress are stored only on your phone. Deleting the app
deletes them. Run2 has no accounts and no cloud sync.

## Maps and location

Run2 uses Apple Maps. When you search for a place, drop a pin, or create a journey, the search
text, the pin's coordinates, or the journey's start and destination are sent to Apple to find
places and road routes. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy
(https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). No health data is part of these requests.

If you tap the button to start from where you are, Run2 asks for your location once, to the
nearest kilometre, and uses it only as the start of the journey: it is sent to Apple once to
look up the place's name, then stored with the journey on your phone. Run2 does not track you.

## Notifications

Run2 shows a notification on your phone when you reach a destination. It is created on the
phone; nothing is sent to a notification server.

## What Run2 does not do

No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party code, no data sold or
shared.

## Children

Run2 is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
